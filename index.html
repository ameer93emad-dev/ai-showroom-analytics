How it works

Eight virtual zones represent an entrance, bathroom displays, tile walls, sample library, vanity islands, curved display rings, and a rotunda. Synthetic agents move through connected zones. The app counts entries and exits, estimates dwell time, and visualizes cumulative engagement.

Not a production camera analytics system. Real deployments require approved hardware integrations, appropriate privacy safeguards, validation, and applicable legal compliance.
